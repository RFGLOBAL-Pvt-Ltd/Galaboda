More Than 20 Years of Trusted Hydration

Founded in May 2004 at Kuledrawatta, Bawwagama in picturesque Nawalapitiya, Galaboda Water began with a straightforward objective: to provide clean, healthy, and accessible drinking water to Sri Lankan families and institutions.

Surrounded by the natural watersheds and rain-drenched hill country of Nawalapitiya, our manufacturing facility operates under registration with the Ministry of Health, Sri Lanka. Over the past two decades, we have evolved from a regional bottler into a recognized national supplier, while preserving our boutique focus on water purity and ethical customer relationships.
